Adolescent Care

You can learn a lot from YouTube and TikTok.

At Healing Hope Psychiatry, we can figure it out together — with you and your family.

Social media provides information on many topics, including about behavioral health conditions, diagnoses, and treatments. It can be overwhelming.

The key is to understand how this information can help you.

The First Visit

We'll sit in a private room together and have a two-way conversation about you. I'll answer your questions, and get to know you. We'll talk about your behavioral health concerns, and I'll also consider your overall medical history and medication history, as applicable. I'll explain what I think is going on, recommend a plan, and explain how that's going to help you.

A Journey, Not a Fixed Plan

This first visit is just the first step in a journey we'll take together — so the initial plan can change.

Trust

My primary relationship is with my patient. Your family is involved, but this process is about you. I'll be honest with you about what's between us and what I share with your family.

Turning 18

An important milestone in your journey is your 18th birthday — we'll map the plan to the important changes that happen then.

The Point

I make diagnoses, recommend treatment, and prescribe medications based on my clinical experience to help you on that journey. Put differently — diagnosis, treatment, and medication aren't the journey. Building the life you want is the journey.

Adolescent Care

Your teen needs help. We're here for them.

At Healing Hope Psychiatry, we help your 16–17 year old on the journey to adulthood.

Your teen is adulting in a different world from you. It's not the world you grew up in, and they may be in a separate world even when they're in your home. It's confusing for you and for them.

The First Visit

I'll meet the family together, and I'll listen to your concerns and the details that are most relevant to you. I will also have private time with your teen so I can get to know them. I'll explain what I think is going on, recommend a plan, and explain how the plan will help.

A Journey, Not a Fixed Plan

This initial visit is the first step in the patient's journey, and the initial plan can change.

Trust

My primary relationship is with my patient. Parents will be involved, but this process is about the teen. I'll be honest with you about what I share with parents and what's private between me and my patient.

Turning 18

An important milestone in this journey is your teen's 18th birthday. The plan will consider the important changes that occur at that time.

The Point

I make diagnoses, recommend treatment, and prescribe medications based on my clinical experience to help you all on that journey. Put differently, diagnosis, treatment, and medication aren't the journey. Helping your teen build their life — that is the journey.
