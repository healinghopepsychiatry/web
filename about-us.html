Ankila Chandran

Psychiatrist, DO, PhD
Marlton, NJ · In-person & telehealth statewide

I'm an osteopathic physician and board-certified psychiatrist with over 20 years of experience. I believe healing works best when it's personal, not procedural — when your care is built around you, not a fifteen-minute slot on someone else's schedule.

Many people I work with are moving through a specific chapter of life that calls for extra support. My approach is milestone-based: we name what you're moving through, build a plan around it, and know what progress and completion look like together. Care isn't open-ended by default — it has direction.

As an osteopathic physician, I look at the whole picture: your body, your history, your relationships, and the life you're actually trying to build — not just a list of symptoms to manage. I hold space for real conversation, not rushed check-ins. I currently see patients via telehealth in NJ, based in Marlton.

Before medical school I got a PhD in Communication, where I learned the many ways people need to feel seen, heard, and felt when facing life's challenges. I pursued a career in medicine to help people more directly. As an osteopathic psychiatrist I have treated patients in a variety of contexts across inpatient and outpatient settings.
