Perinatal Care

Your new baby brings changes that can feel overwhelming.

It's OK to ask for help.

Let's Talk About It

Your situation is new to you. Whatever this is, you don't have to hide it.

Healing Hope Psychiatry can help you with therapy and, if appropriate, with medication.

The First Visit

We'll get to know each other, and I'll learn about your family, your pregnancy, and your medical history. I'll answer your questions, and we'll talk about your goals for your family and your treatment. We'll talk about how you want me to work with your doctor, especially if medicine is involved. I'll explain what I think is going on, recommend a plan, and explain how that's going to help you.

Your Treatment Plan

We design your initial treatment plan around the first year post-delivery, when PPD is most commonly identified and treated. Your experience with PPD may be different, and may not last that long. We'll adapt the plan to your progress.

How I Help You

Based on our discussion, my clinical judgment informs the plan, including whether or when medication is appropriate. The goal is for you to be present for your family.
